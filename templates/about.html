About PDF Darker

PDF Darker is built to make reading and sharing PDFs easier and more comfortable. Bright white documents can be tiring for your eyes, especially at night. Our tool gives you a fast way to convert any PDF into a clean, dark-mode version within seconds.

Every page is processed carefully to darken backgrounds while keeping text clear and readable. Whether you're a student, developer, designer, or someone who prefers dark mode, PDF Darker helps you create a smoother reading experience.

We also offer quick PDF compression so you can reduce file sizes without losing quality. Your documents are processed instantly and never stored. Simplicity, privacy, and speed are at the core of what we do.

PDF Darker is a small project with a simple goal: make your PDFs cleaner, lighter on the eyes, and easier to share.
