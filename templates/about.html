About PDF Darker

We created PDF Darker to offer a clean and comfortable reading experience for PDF users who prefer dark mode. Traditional PDFs with bright white backgrounds can strain your eyes, especially at night. PDF Darker instantly transforms any PDF into a soft, dark-themed version, making reading easier and more enjoyable.

Our Mission

Our goal is simple: provide a fast, free, and effective tool that helps users read PDFs without eye strain. Whether you're a student, developer, designer, or casual reader, PDF Darker ensures your documents look clean and modern.

Why Choose PDF Darker?

  • Instant dark-mode conversion
  • Completely free to use
  • No files stored on server — your data is safe
  • Fast conversion powered by modern rendering tools
  • Simple, clean, and user-friendly interface
